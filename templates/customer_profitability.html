Customer Profitability

Sales, returns, cost of sales and gross profit by customer and by product, calculated from the cost recorded on each sale.

Gross profit is net sales minus the recorded cost of the goods sold. It is not net profit: operating expenses, commissions, delivery and other overheads are not deducted. Gross profit is shown only when every sale and return in the row has a recorded price and cost; otherwise it is shown as Unknown.
