Equivalent Models

Record and review directional replacement options when a product is unavailable. Relationships are explicit Admin entries, never inferred from names or categories.

Recommendations are directional and only appear after an Admin records them. Similar names or shared categories are not treated as equivalence.

An equivalent model does not automatically establish printer and ink compatibility. Use the Ink Dependency module for verified compatibility.
